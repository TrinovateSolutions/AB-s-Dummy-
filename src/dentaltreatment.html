Best Dental Care in Guntur – AB’S Dental & Aesthetics

Welcome to AB’S Dental & Aesthetics, the Best Dental Care in Guntur, where healthy smiles meet advanced technology and compassionate care. We believe every smile is unique, and our mission is to provide personalized dental treatments that restore function, enhance beauty, and ensure long-term oral health.

Our team of expert dentists offers a full range of dental treatments in Guntur, combining modern equipment, painless techniques, and gentle care to make your dental experience comfortable and stress-free.

Comprehensive Dental Treatments at AB’S Dental & Aesthetics

1. General Dentistry

Good oral health begins with prevention. Our general dentistry services include regular dental check-ups, teeth cleaning, scaling and polishing, fillings, fluoride treatments, and oral hygiene education. Early detection and preventive care help you maintain strong and healthy teeth for life.

2. Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile is your best accessory! Our cosmetic dental treatments improve your smile’s appearance through:

  • Teeth Whitening – Brighten and remove stains.
  • Smile Makeover – Personalized transformation.
  • Dental Veneers – Correct chipped, uneven, or discolored teeth.

3. Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Say goodbye to tooth pain! Our painless root canal treatment in Guntur removes infection and saves your natural tooth. Using modern rotary tools and digital X-rays, we ensure fast, effective, and comfortable treatment.

4. Dental Implants

Missing teeth can affect your confidence and oral health. Our dental implant treatments provide a permanent, natural-looking solution using high-quality titanium implants for long-lasting, realistic results.

5. Orthodontic Treatments (Braces & Aligners)

Straighten your smile with our orthodontic solutions, including traditional braces and invisible aligners. Customized plans for all ages ensure comfort and beautiful alignment.

6. Pediatric Dentistry

We make dental visits fun for kids! Our pediatric dental services focus on prevention, cavity care, and developing lifelong healthy oral habits.

7. Gum Treatments

Healthy gums are the foundation of a great smile. Our laser gum therapy and deep cleaning treatments effectively manage bleeding gums, infections, and bad breath.

8. Tooth Extraction & Wisdom Tooth Removal

When extraction is necessary, our skilled dentists perform it with precision and minimal pain, ensuring smooth recovery and care.

9. Dental Crowns & Bridges

Restore damaged or missing teeth with our durable crowns and bridges that blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

10. Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Comprehensive solutions for patients with multiple concerns — combining restorative and cosmetic dentistry to rebuild your smile.
